Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about catalog search, patron accounts, reservations, and renewals.

How do I search the catalog?

Title, author, subject, ISBN

Go to the Catalog page and use the search box and filters.

What is a Patron Account?

Your personal dashboard

A Patron Account lets library members view loans, due dates, holds, fines, and renew books online.

When can I renew a borrowed book?

Renewal rules

Renewals are allowed only when:
- The book is not reserved by another user
- You have not exceeded the maximum renewal limit
- Your account has no heavy fines

What’s the difference between reserve and hold?

Availability

Reserve is used when a book is available. Hold is used to join a queue when the book is checked out.
